Privacy notice

Undiagnosed Magazine. Last updated November 21, 2026.

This covers the magazine: what happens when you give an email address to keep reading. The Undiagnosed app is a separate thing with its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Your email address, and nothing else.

We record the date and time you ticked the box, and which version of the wording you ticked, so we can show what you agreed to.

What we do not collect

  • Nothing about your health. We never ask, and the magazine never reports back on you.
  • Not which pages you read, how long you stayed, or where you stopped.
  • No name, no address, no phone number, no payment details. The magazine is free.
  • No ads, no analytics companies, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies.

Your browser keeps one small marker so the magazine does not ask for your email twice in the same visit. It sits on your device, it clears when you close the browser, and it never reaches us.

What we use it for

One email when a new issue is out. That is the whole list of reasons.

Not app promotions. Not partner offers. Not anything else, now or later.

Who else sees it

Resend, the company that sends our email, stores the list and delivers the messages for us. They process it for us and may not use it for anything of their own.

We do not sell your address. We do not rent, trade, or share it. We do not upload it to Meta, Google, or any other advertising platform.

Stopping

Every issue email has an unsubscribe link, and one tap stops it. You can also write to support@undiagnosedapp.com and ask us to take you off, or to tell you what we hold, or to delete it.

We act on an unsubscribe within 10 business days, usually the same day.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or two years after the last email we sent you, whichever comes first. Then we delete it.
