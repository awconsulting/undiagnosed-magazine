Self-care

It’s specific. Skip “do something creative.”

Do micro-creativity. Tiny. Like, insultingly tiny.

I do mini coloring pages. Not the whole crazy mandala that takes four hours and gives me hella anxiety just looking at it. A small scene. A little corner of something.

The rule

If I can’t finish it in 10 to 15 minutes, I don’t start it.

One page. Three pens. Phone face down.

What it feels like: my shoulders drop about an inch. My jaw unclenches. I’m giving my brain one small thing to hold so it stops holding everything else.
