I have a folder of unhinged comments that made me laugh so hard I had to put my phone down. I have Jessica. I have Jagger, who has one eye, and Drake, who still thinks he runs this place.

That is the real work.

Let your values pick the day, not your symptoms.

There’s a whole therapy built on this. You can’t change the illness, but you can live the way you actually want to live.3, 4

So when my body wakes up and decides today is going to be garbage, I don’t ask what I can get done. I ask what I want this day to be about. Sometimes the answer is rest. Sometimes it’s a bird. Sometimes it’s one text to one person.

My body doesn’t get to pick the theme of the day anymore.
