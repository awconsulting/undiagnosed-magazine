Three things to do before you apply anywhere.

1

Ask the hospital for their financial assistance policy.

Not the billing department. The financial assistance policy. Most hospitals have one and most of them do not advertise it. If you’re uninsured or underinsured, ask before you pay a cent. If they say there isn’t one, ask again in writing.

2

Ask for a payment plan before it goes to collections.

Twenty dollars a month beats a collections account. Hospitals will take something over nothing almost every time. The worst answer is no, and you already have a lot of practice hearing no.

3

Look up the National Association of Free & Charitable Clinics.

It won’t solve a complex specialist problem. It will get you a primary care relationship, and a primary care relationship is how referrals happen.
