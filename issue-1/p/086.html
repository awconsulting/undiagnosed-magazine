My story

Here’s the whole thing, dated, because I am the only one who has kept it.

January 2023

First convulsive episode. No warning. I was just there. Then I wasn’t. My body did something I had never seen it do and I had no language for it. I remember thinking: am I doing this on purpose? That’s the first lie the gray zone tells you. It makes you suspect yourself before anyone else has to.

February 2023

I convulsed on camera during a video EEG. On camera. In a hospital. The read came back clean. Probably anxiety. I have the footage. I have watched it more times than I will admit to anyone, looking for the part where I’m faking it. There isn’t one.
