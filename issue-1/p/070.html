Essay

Do things for pleasure and for mastery.

Pleasure is joy for the sake of joy. Mastery is the tiny win, like finishing the coloring page, arranging three objects on the nightstand, or sending the text you’ve been avoiding for a week. Both count. Neither is frivolous. On low days my brain tells me they’re pointless. I do one anyway. Sometimes the feeling shows up after.

Make meaning while you wait for a diagnosis.

In a 2024 study of 317 adults with inflammatory bowel disease, about 85 percent had asked “why me.”7 The ones who found some kind of meaning, not a cure, not a lesson, just meaning, reported more positive feelings and more growth.

Meaning doesn’t have to be I found my purpose. It can be I made it to 4 p.m. and I laughed at a goose. It can be I built an app for people like me.
