The Quiet Violence of Not Being Believed

There’s a name for what happens in the clinic when your testimony gets dismissed. Philosophers call it

epistemic injustice.

It’s a harm done to you as a knower.

When you show a doctor a video of your body convulsing and they say probably anxiety, you’re being told your account of your own body isn’t a reliable source of knowledge. Your eyes, your phone, your documentation. None of it counts.

A qualitative study of patients with medically unexplained chronic pain found that repeated dismissal changed how people narrated their own pain over time.
