Essay

Engulfment is the one that eats your life. It’s what happens when you’re given a job with no end date and no instructions. But naming it helped me, because I could finally see the difference between living with a question and becoming the question.

I didn’t want to be the question anymore.

What I got wrong about the mission

I thought the mission was the answer. I thought one specialist would hand me a name and the name would make the whole thing make sense. Like the diagnosis would walk in and finally explain me to myself.

Even if the words land on paper someday, my body will still be my body. Paperwork doesn’t do the living.
