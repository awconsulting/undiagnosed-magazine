Column D runs from one to I’m-fine.

I have a folder called Labs. It has subfolders. It has sub-subfolders. I can tell you which hospital has the best parking, which one has the worst phlebotomists, and which neurologist’s office plays smooth jazz so loud you can hear it through the door while you wait to be told “interesting.”

Four years of that. And somewhere in the middle of it, I stopped being a person with a mystery.
