What to Hand a Doctor

1

Put your worst symptom first.

Not your first symptom. Your worst. The thing that is costing you the most right now. If they read one line before their hand goes to the door, that’s the line.

2

Second line: what has changed since the last visit.

Episodes daily, up from weekly. New left-side head pressure. This is the sentence that makes a doctor lean in, because it shows a trajectory. Doctors act on change.
