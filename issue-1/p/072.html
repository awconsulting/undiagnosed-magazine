Essay

Savor the small thing on purpose.

Not gratitude journaling. Not good vibes only. A hot shower. The first sip of cold water. A dog’s ear. That one comment that made you forget you were in pain for thirty seconds.

The whole practice

Turn toward one good thing. Let it land in your body. Twenty seconds.

There’s actual research on this and it’s kind of wild. People with chronic back pain who savored good moments in their day reported less pain.5 People with arthritis who did savoring meditation reported more joy and less of that flat, can’t-feel-anything feeling.6

Small is the only thing that works when big is too heavy.
