Opening letter

Not the poetic kind of 3 a.m. The kind where your hip is screaming, your brain is buzzing, and you’re scrolling through your own camera roll trying to remember what your face looked like before you knew what a flare was.

I don’t have a diagnosis.

Four years. That’s how long. Four years of tests and specialists and that slow nod they do when they’re about to say “interesting” instead of an answer. Four years of it might be this, then it might be that, then let’s try this medication and see. Four years of watching my body fall apart while the paperwork says I’m fine.

I think it’s possible I have FND. Functional Neurological Disorder. My brain and body in a bitter divorce and me as the kid caught in the middle. I’ve thought that for a while now.
