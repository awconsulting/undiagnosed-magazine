The Short List

NORD Undiagnosed Patient Assistance Program

For people referred by the Undiagnosed Diseases Network. Covers out-of-pocket costs. Does not require a diagnosis.

HealthWell Foundation

Copay and premium assistance. Incomes up to 500% of the federal poverty level.

PAN Foundation

Rare and chronic conditions, including travel.

Accessia Health

Rare and chronic conditions, diagnostics included.

Patient Advocate Foundation

Case management and financial aid. They help you argue.

Clinicians Who Care

6,000+ clinicians, filterable by “Listens & Believes Patients.”

Undiagnosed Diseases Network Foundation

Patient navigation and support groups.

National Association of Free & Charitable Clinics

A primary care relationship, which is how referrals happen.
