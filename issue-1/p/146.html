The Group Chat

The most repeated finding in research on peer support for poorly understood illnesses is recognition.

In a study of a support group for people with Long COVID, many navigating diagnostic uncertainty, participants described the group as a space where their illness and struggles were “acknowledged, understood and validated.”

Just being able to reach out after the meetings, it’s so nice, you don’t feel like you’re the Lone Ranger.
I think the most number one most effective aspect is just being in the community and knowing that you’re not alone.
