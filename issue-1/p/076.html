The valley

I’m not going to tell you that you’ll find some magical cure or that one day you’ll look back at this and laugh.

Some days I still cry in the shower. Some days I grieve the body I used to have so hard it feels like a physical ache in my chest.

Some days I look at Jessica and think, she didn’t sign up for this. No name for it. No end date. No pamphlet that tells her what to expect.

And then she brings me ginger ale and threatens to fight a neurologist who hasn’t even given me a diagnosis.

I remember that love doesn’t require a working body. It just requires staying.

It doesn’t require an answer either.
