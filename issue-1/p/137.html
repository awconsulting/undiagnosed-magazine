Where to Find Money

It’s the specialist who doesn’t take your insurance. The labs that aren’t covered because there’s no code for “we don’t know yet.” The second opinion four hours away, which means gas and a hotel and a day you can’t work.

It’s the premium that went up 7.9% this year, from $7,838 to $8,460 for the average person, the steepest jump outside of a pandemic in over a decade, for coverage that keeps saying no. It’s the ER copay you ate because you didn’t have a better option at 2 a.m.

You are not bad with money. You’re paying a tax on not having a name for what’s wrong with you.
