Opening letter

So this is for you. The one reading this in bed. The one who’s tired of being told to stay positive by people who’ve never had to crawl to the bathroom. The one who’s grieving a body they used to have and a life they used to live. The one who’s still waiting for someone in a white coat to say the words.

I see you. I’m you. I want you to feel found.

xoxo,

Ashleigh Ro
