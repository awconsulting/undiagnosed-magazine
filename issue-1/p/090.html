My story

April 2023

A full neurological workup was recommended. It was never done. I didn’t know then that “recommended” and “done” are different words in this system. I know now. I know it the way you know a stove is hot.

February 2025

Liver tests normal in New York. Two weeks later, jaundiced in an Orlando hospital. Same body. Different portal. Nobody put those two facts in the same sentence. I did. At home. Alone.

September 2025

Moved. Lost my insurance. Six portals. Every doctor started at zero. I retold my story so many times that I started trimming it. Not lying. Just cutting the parts that made people’s faces change.
