When someone doubts you

I know I don’t look sick. Trust me, I’ve heard. What’s happening in my body is real even when nobody can see it, and that includes a few doctors. You don’t have to get it. I just need you to believe me when I tell you how I feel. That would mean more than you know.

For the friend who’s pulling away

I miss you. I know I’ve been flaky, and I know that’s annoying to be on the other end of. I never stopped caring. I’m carrying something heavy and it takes a lot out of me. I don’t want to lose you over it. Can we find a version of us that works right now? Even if it’s couch hangs and voice memos for a while.
