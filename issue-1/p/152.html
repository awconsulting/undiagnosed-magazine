Closing note

We’re not besties. I don’t know your birthday. I don’t know what you do for a living or whether you have a dog or whether you’re the kind of person who answers texts right away or the kind who leaves them on read for three days and then sends a paragraph.

But I know what your Tuesday looks like. I know what it costs to make the phone call. I know why you sent the heart emoji instead of the truth. I know the exact sound of a doctor’s voice when they’ve already decided you’re fine, and I know the way your stomach drops when they say it.

We’re the Breakfast Club for the undiagnosed.

Different people, same detention, bound together by something nobody on the outside can see. You didn’t choose this. Neither did I.
