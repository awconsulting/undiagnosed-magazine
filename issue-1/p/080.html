Editor’s letter

I have 31 videos of my body convulsing. Thirty-one. On my phone. In my camera roll. Some are short. Some are long. Some I’ve watched so many times I know exactly when my left arm jerks, when my eyes roll, when the sound of my own breathing changes.

That’s the worst part. Not the convulsions themselves. The videos. Because the videos are proof. Timestamped proof that something is happening to my body. And I have sat in exam rooms with neurologists who watched ten seconds of one video, looked at a clean EEG, and said probably anxiety.

Probably anxiety.
