The Group Chat

One warning from the same research.

Reading everyone else’s worst day with nobody steering can wreck you.

Some groups leave you lighter. Others have you up at 2 a.m. reading strangers’ horror stories, feeling worse than when you joined.

Look for one with a moderator and some rules. Small is good. Best case, somebody with a story like yours goes first.
