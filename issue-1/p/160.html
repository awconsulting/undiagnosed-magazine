The Waiting Room

1Put the dates in order this week. That’s the whole task.
2Put your worst symptom on line one. Not your first one. Your worst.
3Ask “what are we doing next” out loud. Make them answer it.
4Ask them to write down the refusal. Watch what happens.
5Apply to NORD, HealthWell, PAN, Accessia, and the Patient Advocate Foundation. Not every one asks for a diagnosis. Check each one’s rules, then apply.
6Bring a second person to the appointment to take notes. You may check out the moment they say probably anxiety.
