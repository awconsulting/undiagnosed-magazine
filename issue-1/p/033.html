Travel

Prep early.

Start packing weeks ahead so you aren’t wiped out the day you leave. Pack a separate, labeled bag for all medications and keep them in their original pharmacy bottles.

Book a place in the center of your destination so you can easily get back to rest. If a ride fits the budget, it can save energy for the part you came for. Call hotels and airlines directly to verify accessibility details. Don’t just rely on a website.

Use your tools.

Try a Sunflower lanyard. It tells staff at airports and venues that you have a hidden disability and may need help.
