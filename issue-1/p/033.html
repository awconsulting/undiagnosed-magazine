The I’m-not-a-downer intro

I wanted to be upfront about something because I care about our friendship. I’m dealing with a chronic health issue that affects my energy and what I can do. When I say no to plans or need to cancel, please know it’s never personal and I’m not trying to be a downer or dramatic. My body has limits I have to respect. I’d rather be honest with you about that than disappear or pretend I’m fine.

The battery analogy

Imagine your phone starts the day at 100%. Mine starts at maybe 40%, and sometimes 20% on a bad one. Everything I do, like getting dressed or having a conversation, drains that battery way faster than yours. I’m not choosing to be tired or lazy. My battery just runs out faster. That’s why I have to plan and rest so carefully.
