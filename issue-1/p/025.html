Self-care

I sit on my balcony and look for birds. I don’t have binoculars. I don’t have a bird app. I sit there and I look and I wait. My brain has a harder time spiraling when it’s busy watching a tree.

Arranging a few small objects on your nightstand counts too. Moving three things into a row is a decision you can make when you can’t make any others. You can see the win.
