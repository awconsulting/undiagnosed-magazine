When someone doubts you

I know my illness isn’t obvious from the outside, and it’s frustrating that others can’t see it. But even without visible signs, my symptoms are real and affect my daily life. I’m not asking you to fully understand it, just to believe me when I tell you what I’m experiencing. That would mean a lot.

For the friend who’s pulling away

I’ve noticed we haven’t connected as much, and I want to be honest with you. I know my illness might make me seem unreliable sometimes. It’s not because I don’t care about you. I do. I’m just trying to manage something really hard. I’d love to find a new way to stay in each other’s lives, even if it looks different than before.
