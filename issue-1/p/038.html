The I’m-not-a-downer intro

Hey, I want to tell you something because I like you and I want to keep liking you for a long time. I have a chronic health thing going on and my energy isn’t what it used to be. So if I bail on plans, it’s never about you. I’m not being dramatic. Some days my body is out of gas by noon. I’d rather tell you that than slowly disappear and make you guess.
