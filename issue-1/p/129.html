What to Hand a Doctor

Ask them to document the refusal.

If they won’t order the test, say: Can you note in my chart that I asked for this and it was declined? Watch how fast the conversation changes. Sometimes the test gets ordered.

Write down what they said.

Before you leave the parking lot, note the date, the doctor’s name and the exact words. Next time, nobody gets to say it never happened.
