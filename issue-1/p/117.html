How to Find a Doctor Who Actually Cares

Being dismissed by a doctor is an injury. Searching for one who listens can feel like dating in a city where everyone has already decided you are not worth their time.

01

Use the directories that already exist.

There is a free, community-built resource called Clinicians Who Care, a directory of more than 6,000 clinicians across dozens of specialties, filterable by location and by tags like “Listens & Believes Patients” and “Patient Advocate.”

No guarantee, but it was built by people who know what dismissal feels like.
