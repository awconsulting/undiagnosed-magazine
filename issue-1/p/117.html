How to Find a Doctor Who Actually Cares

03

Prepare two or three concerns, not twenty.

Dr. Bayo Curry-Winchell, a physician who was dismissed for years before her own lupus diagnosis, recommends bringing two or three primary concerns and describing specifically how symptoms are affecting your daily life.

I cannot work. I cannot drive. I cannot take care of my children.

That is the language that lands.
