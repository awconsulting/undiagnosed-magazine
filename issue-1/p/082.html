Editor’s letter

I’ve made creators $175 million through my work. I’ve worked with MrBeast and Post Malone. I know how to make people see things. And none of it mattered when my body started failing. It robbed me of damn near everything. The work. The identity. The feeling of having made it. And I still spent four years watching doctors look at me like I was a problem they didn’t have time to solve.

I built the app because I needed it. This magazine is for the rest of it. What to do when advocacy becomes suffocating. How to find a doctor who actually cares. How to survive the American healthcare system while it eats your savings alive. How to ask for help when you have spent so long being the one who holds it all together that you have forgotten how to be held.
