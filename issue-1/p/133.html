When Advocacy Becomes Suffocating

There is a moment that comes for everyone in the gray zone. You have been strong for so long. You have made the calls. You have sent the portal messages. You have rehearsed the story. You have held the videos up to doctors who didn’t want to watch them. And then one day, you cannot do it anymore.

Your body and your spirit have run out of the energy it takes to be your own full-time advocate.
