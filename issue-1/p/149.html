The Group Chat

You know the look. You’re halfway through explaining your symptoms and the other person’s face goes polite.

In a room full of people who are also still waiting on answers, nobody makes that face. They nod before you finish.

Researchers studied a Long COVID support group, a lot of them still undiagnosed.

People kept calling it the place where their illness was “acknowledged, understood and validated.”

Just being able to reach out after the meetings, it’s so nice, you don’t feel like you’re the Lone Ranger.
I think the most number one most effective aspect is just being in the community and knowing that you’re not alone.
